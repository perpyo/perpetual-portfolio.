03 / A little about me

Curiosity is my through line.

Perpetual, but you can call me Perpy.

I’m an Ottawa-based creative who likes connecting the idea, the visual, and the story.

My interest in content led me to Interactive Media Design at Algonquin College and to work across branded content, retail, and independent video production.

Running Hair by Perpy also keeps me close to the practical side of creative work: communicating with clients, managing bookings, organizing content, and following through on details.
